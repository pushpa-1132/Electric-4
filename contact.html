Frequently Asked Questions

Before contacting us, you might find answers to common questions below:

What age range are your electric ATVs suitable for?

Our 24V models are perfect for kids ages 3-8, while our 36V ATVs are designed for ages 8 and up, including teens and adults.

How long does the battery last?

Battery life varies by model and usage, but typically 1-2 hours of continuous riding. Charging time is approximately 8-12 hours.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, all our electric ATVs come with manufacturer warranties. Specific coverage details are provided with each product.

Can I use these ATVs on public roads?

These are off-road vehicles designed for private property use. Check your local regulations regarding ATV usage.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, we offer shipping throughout the United States. Shipping costs and times vary by location.
