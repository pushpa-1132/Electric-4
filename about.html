About Electric ATV Store

Pioneering the Future of Off-Road Adventure

Our Story

Founded with a passion for adventure and a commitment to sustainability, Electric ATV Store has become a trusted name in battery-powered off-road vehicles. We specialize in electric ATV 4 wheelers that combine cutting-edge technology with environmental responsibility.

Our journey began when we recognized the need for quieter, cleaner, and safer alternatives to traditional gas-powered ATVs. Today, we're proud to offer a comprehensive range of electric quads, from kids' 24V models to powerful 36V ATVs for experienced riders.

Our Mission

We're dedicated to making off-road adventures accessible, safe, and environmentally friendly for everyone. Our mission is to:

  • Provide high-quality electric ATV 4 wheelers that deliver exceptional performance
  • Ensure the safety of riders through advanced features like remote kill switches and speed limiters
  • Promote eco-friendly transportation alternatives that reduce carbon emissions
  • Offer outstanding customer service and support throughout your ownership experience
  • Make electric off-road vehicles accessible to families and riders of all skill levels

Why Choose Electric?

Electric ATVs represent the future of off-road recreation. They offer numerous advantages over traditional gas-powered vehicles:

  • Environmental Benefits: Zero emissions and reduced noise pollution
  • Lower Operating Costs: No fuel expenses and minimal maintenance requirements
  • Safety Features: Advanced parental controls and safety mechanisms
  • Quiet Operation: Enjoy nature without disturbing wildlife or neighbors
  • Instant Torque: Electric motors deliver immediate power for better performance

Our Product Range

We carefully select and test every electric ATV in our collection to ensure it meets our high standards for quality, safety, and performance. Our range includes:

  • 24V Electric ATVs: Perfect for beginners and younger riders, featuring adjustable speeds and parental controls
  • 36V Electric Quads: High-performance models for experienced riders seeking more power and range
  • 4x4 Electric ATVs: All-terrain vehicles built to handle challenging off-road conditions
  • Side by Side Models: Two-seater options for shared adventures and family bonding
  • Accessories & Parts: Complete range of safety gear, replacement parts, and upgrades

Quality & Safety Commitment

Safety is our top priority. Every electric ATV we offer includes essential safety features such as:

  • Remote kill switches for parental control
  • Adjustable speed limiters
  • Safety belts and protective frames
  • LED lighting for visibility
  • Sturdy construction and quality materials

Customer-Focused Service

We believe that purchasing an electric ATV is just the beginning of your relationship with us. Our team is dedicated to providing:

  • Expert guidance to help you choose the right model
  • Comprehensive product information and specifications
  • Responsive customer support for questions and concerns
  • Technical assistance and troubleshooting
  • Access to replacement parts and accessories

Partner Network
